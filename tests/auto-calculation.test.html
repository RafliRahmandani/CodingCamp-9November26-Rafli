Test: Auto-Calculation of Total Belanja (Task 8.3)

This test verifies that the Total Belanja field auto-calculates based on:

  • Kuantitas (Quantity)
  • Harga Barang (Unit Price)
  • Ongkir (Shipping)
  • Sisa Bayar (Remaining Payment)

Formula: Total Belanja = (Kuantitas × Harga Barang) + Ongkir + Sisa Bayar

Test 1: Basic Calculation

Expected: (2 × 50.000) + 10.000 + 5.000 = 115.000

Test 2: Decimal Values

Expected: (3 × 15.500,50) + 7.500 + 2.000 = 54.051,50
